## 2. Cannibalization Analysis & Resolution Proposals

### Issue A: `/calculator` vs `/tools/focus-time-calculator` (Critical Overlap)
- **Problem Statement:**  
  Currently, both `/calculator` (Study Efficiency & Focus Time ROI Calculator) and `/tools/focus-time-calculator` (Focus Time Calculator: Deep Work & Pomodoro) compete for the exact same search queries: `focus time calculator`, `attention residue calculator`, and `study efficiency calculator`. This splits backlink equity, causes Google to alternate indexation between the two URLs, and degrades rankings for both.
- **Root Cause in Architecture:**  
  `/calculator` is positioned at the root level, whereas all other 5 interactive study tools reside cleanly under the `/tools/` subfolder path (`/tools/study-timetable-generator`, `/tools/daily-routine-generator`, etc.). Furthermore, `/calculator` runs a large 64KB engine with PDF export and scenario saving, while `/tools/focus-time-calculator` runs an 8KB lightweight engine.
- **Resolution Options:**
  - **Proposal 1 (Recommended — Architectural Unification with 301 Redirect):**  
    Merge `/calculator` into `/tools/focus-time-calculator`. Move the advanced `FocusRoiCalculator` engine into `/tools/focus-time-calculator`, and set up an HTTP 301 permanent redirect from `https://lifetrackpro.in/calculator` to `https://lifetrackpro.in/tools/focus-time-calculator`.  
    *Benefit:* Concentrates all topical authority, backlinks, and user traffic onto a single authoritative URL under the `/tools/` cluster.  
    *Target Primary Keyword:* `focus time calculator`
  - **Proposal 2 (Differentiate by Intent & Query Clustering):**  
    If both URLs must remain active, strictly differentiate their keyword clusters and search intent:  
    1. **`/calculator`** -> Primary Keyword: **`study distraction calculator`** (Secondary: `attention residue calculator`, `study time lost to phone`). Angles solely on the financial/time loss caused by phone notifications and recovery lag.  
    2. **`/tools/focus-time-calculator`** -> Primary Keyword: **`focus time calculator`** (Secondary: `deep work calculator`, `net study hours calculator`, `pomodoro block calculator`). Angles solely on calculating net daily focus hours and sizing 50/10 vs 25/5 Pomodoro blocks.

---

### Issue B: `/tools/study-hours-calculator` vs `/tools/study-timetable-generator` vs `/tools/weekly-study-planner`
- **Problem Statement:**  
  All three tools deal with study scheduling and planning, creating potential semantic confusion if terms like "study planner" or "timetable" are used interchangeably in titles and H1s.
- **Resolution (Distinct Semantic Separation):**
  - **`/tools/study-hours-calculator`** owns **Workload & Feasibility**: "How many total hours and daily study hours do I need to finish 40 chapters in 60 days?" -> Primary: `study hours calculator`
  - **`/tools/study-timetable-generator`** owns **Daily Hour-by-Hour Execution**: "Create my 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM subject schedule with Pomodoro intervals." -> Primary: `study timetable generator`
  - **`/tools/weekly-study-planner`** owns **7-Day Subject Balancing**: "How do I allocate Physics, Chemistry, and Math across Monday to Saturday with Sunday for mock test analysis?" -> Primary: `weekly study planner`

---

### Issue C: `/tools/daily-routine-generator` vs `/tools/study-timetable-generator`
- **Problem Statement:**  
  Users and search engines could conflate "routine" and "timetable".
- **Resolution:**  
  - **`/tools/daily-routine-generator`** focuses on the **24-hour circadian lifestyle schedule** (wake-up time, classes/college, meal buffers, gym/walk, sleep window, generic study blocks). -> Primary: `daily routine generator for students`
  - **`/tools/study-timetable-generator`** focuses strictly on **academic subject revision intervals & Pomodoro sprints**. -> Primary: `study timetable generator`

---

### Issue D: `/features/spaced-repetition` vs Seed Keyword `anki alternative india`
- **Problem Statement:**  
  `anki alternative india` is a high-intent commercial comparison query. Currently, `/features/spaced-repetition` mentions Anki, but neither has a dedicated comparison table nor clean secondary keyword optimization.
- **Resolution:**  
  Assign `anki alternative india` as the #1 secondary keyword for `/features/spaced-repetition`. Maintain `spaced repetition flashcards app` as the primary keyword to capture broad discovery, with on-page comparison copy positioning LifeTrack Pro as the mobile-first, zero-friction Indian student alternative to clunky desktop Anki setup.

### B. Contradictory Claims Across Site
1. **"Zero Ads / 100% Ad-Free" vs Google AdSense / AdMob:**  
   - `/features/pomodoro-timer` and `/features/study-rooms` claim *"zero ads"*, *"100% ad-free"*, and *"zero banner ads"*.  
   - However, `src/app/layout.js:91` loads Google AdSense client ID (`ca-pub-3825001060710787`), and both `/ads.txt` and `/app-ads.txt` are live on production.  
   - *Recommendation:* Clarify copy to state *"No intrusive banner ads interrupting your focus timer sessions"* rather than an absolute *"100% ad-free platform"*, avoiding trust erosion.
2. **"100% Local / Zero Cloud Dependence" vs "Supabase Cloud Sync & Real-Time Multiplayer":**  
   - Feature copy on multiple pages emphasizes *"100% local storage with zero cloud dependence"*, while FAQ, Privacy Policy, and Study Rooms highlight *"Supabase encrypted cloud sync"* and *"real-time multiplayer rooms"*.  
   - *Recommendation:* Align messaging to: *"Offline-first architecture: study timers and flashcards work 100% offline without an account, with optional secure Supabase cloud sync for study rooms and multi-device backup."*

    "ratingValue": "4.9",

- **Action Required in Phase 2:** Immediately remove `aggregateRating` until verified, authentic Play Store reviews can be dynamically fed via official APIs without violating Google Rich Results Guidelines.

### E. Title Template Double-Branding & Character Overflow
- In `src/app/layout.js`, `title.template` is configured as `'%s | LifeTrack Pro'`.
- When individual page files specify titles that already include `| LifeTrack Pro` or `– LifeTrack Pro`, Next.js appends the template suffix, causing:
  - `Study Efficiency & Focus Time ROI Calculator – LifeTrack Pro | LifeTrack Pro` (76 chars)
  - `Free Daily Routine Generator for Students | 24-Hour Lifestyle & Schedule Planner | LifeTrack Pro` (96 chars)
  - `Free Study Timetable Generator for Students | AI Subject & Exam Planner | LifeTrack Pro` (87 chars)
- In Phase 2, we will ensure page titles pass only the raw prefix string to the template OR specify `title.absolute` to guarantee strictly <= 60 characters with the brand occurring exactly once at the end.

---

### F. Duplicate Sections / DOM Redundancy
- In `src/app/page.js` (lines 144–155), marquee ticker DOM items are duplicated identically within the same container for CSS scrolling. We should ensure `aria-hidden="true"` is added to the duplicated track to prevent screen readers and search bots from parsing duplicate content.
