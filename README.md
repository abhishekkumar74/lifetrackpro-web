## 💡 Why LifeTrack Pro?

Most serious aspirants juggle **4–5 fragmented apps** every day: a standalone Pomodoro clock, an Anki flashcard viewer, a habit counter, and spreadsheets for syllabus completion. This creates notification fatigue, high context switching, and fragmented data.

**LifeTrack Pro replaces all of them with one disciplined Study OS**, purpose-built for India's toughest competitive exams (**IIT JEE, NEET UG, UPSC CSE**) and high-intensity tech placement preparation.

---

## ⚡ Core Productivity Systems

### 1. Smart Pomodoro Focus Timer with Ambient Soundscapes
Customizable focus blocks (25/5 or 50/10 deep work) accompanied by soothing acoustic soundscapes (432Hz binaural alpha waves, gentle rainfall, café hum, and lo-fi beats). 

<p align="center">
  <img src="public/assets/images/app_focus_timer_active.png" width="460" alt="LifeTrack Pro Pomodoro Focus Timer" style="border-radius: 18px; box-shadow: 0 12px 36px rgba(0,0,0,0.12);" />
</p>

- **Persistent Foreground Service:** Keeps timers ticking and audio flowing even when your phone screen is locked.
- **Session Auto-Logging:** Every completed sprint is automatically credited to your daily subject focus chart.
- **Distraction Interception:** Fullscreen deep focus mode discourages impulsive social media checks.

---

### 2. Habit Streaks with Emergency Freeze Shields
Consistency beats intensity. Build non-negotiable daily routines with automated safety nets.

<p align="center">
  <img src="public/assets/images/app_habit_streak.png" width="460" alt="LifeTrack Pro Habit Streaks and Freeze Shields" style="border-radius: 18px; box-shadow: 0 12px 36px rgba(0,0,0,0.12);" />
</p>

- **Streak Freeze Shields:** Missed a study session due to travel or illness? Automated freeze tokens absorb the gap without breaking your multi-month streak.
- **Routine Micro-Tasks:** Break complex exam preparation into daily minimum commitments (e.g., 50 MCQs, 2 Answer Writing sets).
- **Streak Milestones:** Earn discipline badges as you pass 7-day, 30-day, and 100-day consistency records.

---

### 3. Syllabus Milestone Trackers (JEE • NEET • UPSC)
Visual progress trackers mapped directly to NCERT and target competitive syllabi.

<p align="center">
  <img src="public/assets/images/app_learn_syllabus.png" width="460" alt="LifeTrack Pro Exam Syllabus Tracker" style="border-radius: 18px; box-shadow: 0 12px 36px rgba(0,0,0,0.12);" />

- **Chapter-Level Checklists:** Track syllabus completion percentages across Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, and General Studies.
- **Exam Countdown Clocks:** Keep exact target dates front and center to pace your revision schedules effectively.
- **Weak Topic Tagging:** Pin difficult chapters for targeted spaced repetition review.

### 4. Deep Work Analytics & Productivity Heatmaps
Turn your preparation into actionable data. Understand exactly where your study hours go.

## 🔒 Privacy & Architecture Principles

- **100% Offline-First:** All cards, timers, habits, and syllabus notes persist locally on your device with zero cloud latency.
- **Zero Commercial Ads:** No disruptive banners, no pop-up video interruptions during focus sprints.
- **Zero Data Mining:** Your study habits belong exclusively to you. We do not sell user data to advertising brokers.
- **Battery-Optimized:** Built with native foreground services engineered to draw minimal battery on Android 8.0+.
